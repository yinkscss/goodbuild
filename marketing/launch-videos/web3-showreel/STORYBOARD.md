# Showreel storyboard

**Concept (one line): "One dot, zoomed through": a single violet dot is the full stop of "Vibe code any Web3 app." and every shot is that dot growing, turning into the next shape and becoming the next background, until it is the full stop again.**

1920x1080, 15 s, 120 BPM (0.5 s per beat, 30 beats), Geist only, violet `#592E81` + pink `#FF85D9` + lilac `#E2D2F0` + black and white. One camera language: zoom-through (the shape expands until it fills the frame and becomes the next scene's colour).

| Beats | Time | Technique | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 (bar 1) | 0-2 s | **Kinetic type (hook)** | Frame 0 is the end title: "Vibe code any Web3 app." Each word pulses on its beat, then all words are sucked into the full stop, which zooms through to violet. |
| 4-8 (bar 2) | 2-4 s | **Shape morphing** | White circle, morphs to a prompt bar, types "Savings circle for my club", Build it pill splits off. |
| 8-12 (bar 3) | 4-6 s | **2D to 3D** | The white tile gains depth, tilts and becomes a cube; it turns to "Testnet" then "Mainnet" (test on testnet first). |
| 12-16 (bar 4) | 6-8 s | **Data / UI** | The cube lands as a card: Backend, "Your app saves data", a line draws itself, entries count up, tooltip. |
| 16-20 (bar 5) | 8-10 s | **Kinetic type (the problem and the answer)** | On pink: "Subscriptions" is struck through and thrown out; "Pay as you go." then "From $0.25 in G$." |
| 20-24 (bar 6) | 10-12 s | **UI** | Credit card: amount scrubs $0.25 to $5.00, "Pay with G$", "One signature". |
| 24-28 (bar 7, strongest) | 12-14 s | **Morph + type** | Toggle flips Celo Sepolia to Celo on the beat, the pill floods the frame, "Live on mainnet." lands word by word. |
| 28-30 (half bar) | 14-15 s | **End title** | Words return from the full stop; final frame equals frame 0 (loop). |

Audio: kick on every beat, riser from beat 20 into bar 7, a hit on every scene change, synthesised in code (Web Audio OfflineAudioContext).
